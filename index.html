Price checks on Upstox

    
A year of daily candles per stock tests the letters the earnings query can't. N: within 15% of the 52-week high. L: weighted 3, 6, 9 and 12-month return beats Nifty 500 by 10 points or more. S: up-day volume beats down-day volume by 1.1× over 50 sessions. A stock passes when it clears N and L.

    

      

      

      
The token is kept on this device until 3:30 AM IST, when Upstox tokens expire, and is sent only to api.upstox.com. Lists from canslim_screener.py already carry instrument keys.

    

    

      
      
    

    

    

  


  

    
4
Research and shortlist

    
Claude reviews each company's growth plans, capex, order book, guidance, institutional interest and recent news, then scores the outlook. The final score weighs fundamentals 30%, price checks 30% and outlook 40%, using whichever parts have run. With live web search on, Claude searches the last 90 days of news for each stock. You can also paste headlines or concall notes into any stock's notes.

    

      

      

        

      

        
        
        
      

      
Optional and paid: everything else on this page is free, and without a key each card still shows its headlines and a free headline tone. The key goes only from this browser to api.anthropic.com. Live search runs one request per stock, with up to 3 searches each ($10 per 1,000 searches plus tokens). With search off, stocks go in batches of 6 using Claude's own knowledge, which has a cutoff date.
