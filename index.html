Research and shortlist

    
Claude reviews each company's growth plans, capex, order book, guidance, institutional interest and recent news, then scores the outlook. The final score is 40% fundamentals from the screen and 60% outlook. With live web search on, Claude searches the last 90 days of news for each stock. You can also paste headlines or concall notes into any stock's notes.

    

      

      

        

      

        
        
      

      
The key goes only from this browser to api.anthropic.com. Live search runs one request per stock, with up to 3 searches each ($10 per 1,000 searches plus tokens). With search off, stocks go in batches of 6 using Claude's own knowledge, which has a cutoff date.
